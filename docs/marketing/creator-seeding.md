# Seeding 20 creators

Poocho grows the way NGL did: through links on Instagram stories. New visitors only stay when they see real activity, so the first job is getting 20 creators to post their Poocho link, once a week, for a month. Everything else in the launch amplifies that.

## Who to pick

Look for micro-creators with **5,000 to 50,000 followers** who are **18 or over** and who already post story Q&As. The best fits for Poocho are Hindi-belt creators (Lucknow, Kanpur, Jaipur, Indore, Patna, Delhi NCR) and college niches: placements, hostel life, entrance exams, campus fests, study vlogs, fitness and fashion on a student budget.

Three signals matter more than follower count. First, they already use the Instagram question sticker, so the habit exists. Second, their comments read like a community rather than giveaway spam. Third, they reply to people, because hosts who answer get asked more.

Skip anyone who appears to be under 18 or whose audience is mostly school students. Poocho is adults-only, and the FTC's case against NGL turned on marketing to minors.

**Where to find them:** Instagram search and hashtags such as `#lucknowcreator`, `#collegelife`, `#placementprep`, `#hostellife`, `#studygram`, `#delhiuniversity`; the "Suggested for you" chain from one good fit; college fest pages and the creators they tag; and creators who've posted "NGL" or "ask me anything" stories (search the phrase in reels).

Send about 40 DMs to land 20. Log every one in [creators.csv](./creators.csv).

## The offer

Be exact about what exists today, and keep promises small enough to keep.

- **Founding Creator status.** Poocho is free now. When paid plans launch, Founding Creators get Pro free for life, in writing.
- **Your own card style.** We design a sixth story-card template in their colours with their name on it, and ship it to their account first.
- **A direct line.** WhatsApp access to the founder for feature requests, with their name in the changelog when one ships.

**The ask:** one "Ask me" story with their Poocho link each week for four weeks, starting the week of 9 November, and answering at least three questions as story cards each time.

## Follow-ups

**After 3 days, no reply:** "Hi {name}, just bumping this in case it got buried. Happy to set up your link for you in a minute if that's easier. No worries if it's not for you!"

**They say yes:** "Amazing, welcome aboard! 🎉 Claim your link here: https://poocho.vercel.app/start (Google sign-in, 30 seconds). Tell me your handle and your two favourite colours and I'll start on your card style. For your story, use this version of your link so we can see it working: https://poocho.vercel.app/@{handle}?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=story&utm_campaign=seed"

**They ask "is it really anonymous?":** "Yes. You see the question and when it arrived, never the asker's name, phone or location. Poocho keeps limited technical data for 30 days only to stop abuse, and never shows it to you. It's all written down here: https://poocho.vercel.app/pledge"

**They ask "what if I get hate?":** "It's filtered before it reaches you. You choose Relaxed, Standard or Strict, block any words you like, and the Filtered folder stays closed unless you open it. You can pause your link any time."

## Weekly rhythm with each creator

Every Monday, send their weekly nudge with one suggested prompt from [the ideas page](https://poocho.vercel.app/ideas/story-questions) matched to their niche. Midweek, reshare one of their answer cards from Poocho's account (with permission). On Sunday, log in [creators.csv](./creators.csv) whether they posted and roughly how many questions they got (they can tell you, or you can see the host count on `/admin` move).

If someone posts nothing for two weeks running, thank them and free up the slot for the next creator on the list.

## Safety for creators

Send every Founding Creator this note once: "If a question ever upsets you, you never have to answer it. Delete it, block the words, or pause your link from Settings. Report anything threatening to us and we'll act within 24 hours. And if things feel heavy, Tele-MANAS is free and confidential, day and night, on 14416."
