# Content calendar: Poocho's own channels

Three reels a week on Instagram for four weeks, one carousel, WhatsApp status copy, and a weekly build-in-public post. Every reel ends on a story with the link sticker pointing at the reels link in [utm-links.md](./utm-links.md).

**House style.** Shoot vertically on a phone, in natural light, with text on screen in the brand's ballpoint blue and haldi yellow. Hook in the first second, no longer than 20 seconds, captions on, and the Poocho logo only in the last two seconds. Keep the tone of the pledge: honest and warm, never mocking a person. Never show a real user's question without their written permission.
