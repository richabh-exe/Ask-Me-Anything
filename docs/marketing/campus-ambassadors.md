# Campus ambassadors

Week 3 onward. Ambassadors run live seniors' AMAs before placements and fests, which puts Poocho in front of a whole batch at once and gives first-years a reason to claim their own links.

## Who

Students **aged 18 or over** at colleges and universities, ideally a society lead, fest organiser or placement-cell volunteer who already runs events. Start with three or four campuses in Lucknow and Delhi NCR. Never recruit at schools or coaching centres where students may be under 18.

## What they do

Each ambassador runs **one seniors' AMA a month** and shares their tagged campus link (see [utm-links.md](./utm-links.md)) in their society and batch groups.

## What they get

A Poocho T-shirt and stickers after their first AMA; a signed certificate of recognition after two; a public leaderboard on Poocho's Instagram ranking ambassadors by questions answered at their AMAs; and Founding Creator status (Pro free for life when it launches).

## The seniors' AMA, step by step

1. **Pick the moment.** The week before placements, internship season, fest registrations or first-year orientation.
2. **Make the link.** A senior (or the society) claims a link with a prompt like "Ask the final years anything about placements". Set it to Anonymous and Standard filtering.
3. **Share it two days ahead** in the junior batch's WhatsApp groups and on the society's Instagram story, with the campus link for anyone who wants their own.
4. **Run it live.** Put the inbox on a projector (filtered questions never show there), pick the most-asked themes, and have two or three seniors answer out loud.
5. **Publish afterwards.** Seniors save their best answers as story cards and post them, so the batch that missed it can catch up and ask more.
6. **Report back.** The ambassador sends the founder the number of questions and two photos (with everyone's consent) for the leaderboard post.
